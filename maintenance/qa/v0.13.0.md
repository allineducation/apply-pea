# v0.13.0 applicant orientation — September 28, 2026 (pre-deploy, local)

## Changes

- **Participar: 3-step orientation** (`ol.applicant-steps`) sits above the existing “three steps to join” list, which holds the primary interest-form CTA. It is a vertical timeline with connectors on phones and three columns from 720px up. It is static and does not track progress.
- **Help card** (`aside.help-card`) sits directly after the join steps, beside the Apply / Zoom CTAs. It has tap-to-email and tap-to-call links with a 48px minimum height.
- **Commitment + certificate** on Participar are now one “Program details and commitment” accordion (`details.accordion-item`). The accordion helper takes an optional third element, rendered as a small note.
- **Audience card icons** changed to 📝 Applicants · 🎓 Participants · 🤝 Alumni, as requested.
- New ES/EN copy is in `copy.js` → `applicant.orientH`, `orient`, `helpText`, `helpEmail`, `helpCall` and `detailsH`. It is **pending Danny’s copy approval**.

## Kept as-is (already in v0.12.0, brand-mapped)

Sticky blurred header, pill language toggle, audience card styling, safe-area bottom bar and FAQ accordions were already in place. Requested slate hex values (`#0f172a`, `#475569`, `#e2e8f0`…) were **not** introduced; the existing palette tokens are equivalent and on brand. The toggle stays **ES | EN** (Spanish-first, brand §5.3). The quick-action bar stays `position: fixed`, because it is outside the scroll container.

## Wording deviations from the request

“Submit Form” describes the **interest form**. Approved copy says it is not an application and there is no selection process, so no step says “application”. Step 1 reads “review who can join and the time commitment”, not “school district”: no district restriction is published. The English help line keeps the requested “Questions about applying?”.

## Verification

- Parity: 427 ES / 427 EN strings, vocabulary clean. `node --check` passes on `app.js`. `git diff --check` is clean.
- Playwright (Chromium, local `http.server`): `#participar` and `#inicio`, ES and EN, at 320, 390 and 1100px. No horizontal overflow and no console errors. 3 step items render, and the orientation comes before the join steps. The help card is the next sibling of the join steps. Both help links measure 48px tall. 2 accordions render on Participar.

## Limits

This was not tested on physical devices and no axe audit was run. Production is not published until merged to `main`.
