# v0.12.0 UI/UX polish — September 28, 2026 (pre-deploy, local)

## Changes

- Tokens (`--color-*`, `--radius-*`, `--shadow-*`) at the end of `hub.css`, mapped to the palette: text `#333333`, secondary `#707070`, borders `#E7E7E7`, active border Blue `#053CAA`. Shadows are Blue-tinted. Headings use -0.02em tracking and Lato 900.
- Sticky, translucent header (a solid-white fallback applies where `backdrop-filter` is unsupported). The menu moved into the header, and `scroll-padding-top` keeps focused headings visible below it.
- ES | EN pill toggle: two buttons with `aria-pressed` and `lang`. Focus stays on the active pill, and `#live` announces the change.
- Floating bottom bar (`nav.quickbar`, fixed): translucent, `padding-bottom: calc(.75rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`, icon above label, 48px minimum targets. Emoji icons were kept at Danny's direction.
- Home audience router: three cards (Applicants → `#participar`, Participants → `#calendario`, Alumni → `#contacto`). New ES/EN copy is in `copy.js` (`applicant.audienceH`, `applicant.audience`) and is **pending Danny's copy approval**.
- “Is PEA a fit for me?” is now an accordion (first item open), and the FAQ carries the same accordion classes. Search/filter behaviour is unchanged.
- `.skeleton-loader` + `@keyframes pulse` are reserved and connected to nothing.
- Brand exception (radius 16px, pills, soft shadows, blur) is recorded in the `hub.css` header and README.

## Verification

- Parity: 416 ES / 416 EN strings, vocabulary clean, 18 module descriptions. `node --check` passes for all three JS files. `git diff --check` is clean.
- Playwright (Chromium, local `http.server`): `#inicio`, `#preguntas` and `#calendario` in ES and EN at 320, 390 and 1100px. No horizontal overflow and no console errors in any combination. The header stays at top 0 after scrolling. Header height is 64px (mobile) / 68px (desktop), and the bar is 80px / 69px.
- Keyboard: the first Tab reaches the skip link, and Enter moves focus to `#main`. The EN pill sets `lang=en`, keeps focus on the pill, and the live region reads “Page in English”. Enter opens the second eligibility accordion. The card hover transform is `translateY(-2px)`. The Alumni card routes to `#contacto`.
- Found and fixed during QA: the pill toggle was hidden by the older mobile rule `.band-r>div{display:none}`. The inactive pill and the menu links inherited white text from `.band a/button`.
- Contrast (calculated): white on Blue 9.40, Blue on white 9.40, `#707070` on white 4.95, Blue on `#F7F7F7` above 8.

## Limits

This was not tested on physical iOS or Android devices, so the safe-area inset was checked by CSS only. No axe audit was run. The Netlify headers and redirects don't apply to local hosting. No forms were submitted and no messages were sent. Production is not published until this branch is merged to `main`.
